Printing on Physical Certificates:
  1. Deploy your certificate page to GitHub Pages or Vercel (see README.md).
  2. Enter your live public URL above and click Generate QR.
  3. Click Download High-Res PNG and paste the image onto your certificate canvas (Canva, Photoshop, Word, or LaTeX) at 1.5 × 1.5 inches (or ~3.8cm).
  4. Test scan the printed certificate or computer screen using any iPhone / Android camera app. It will open Mansi Suryawanshi's record immediately!
